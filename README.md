# 🗣 Vaani

**वाणी** — speech. The voice as it is actually used, not the language on paper.

Seventy-two days that end with you holding a **ten-minute unscripted conversation in English**
with a stranger, on a topic you did not prepare, without freezing and without translating in
your head.

---

## What this is not

- Not a grammar course. Grammar appears only where it blocks the mouth.
- Not a hundred days with twenty documents each. **One document a day**, plus a hub.
- Not reading practice wearing a speaking costume. Every day ends with your voice recorded.
- Not a coding project. There is no code anywhere in this repository, by design (ADR-0001).

## The shape

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Days | 72 — six on, one off, twelve weeks |
| A session | five fixed blocks, one sitting, roughly half an hour |
| A day | one hub + one part document. Two parts on rehearsal days. Never three. |
| Cost | nothing. A phone recorder and a wall. |
| Partner | not required. Every rep has a solo form. |

## Start here

1. Read [`docs/00_MASTER_PLAN.md`](docs/00_MASTER_PLAN.md) — the contract. The day map is §8.
2. Read [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) if an assistant is going to write days with you.
3. Open [`days/day-01-baseline-and-the-log/LESSON.md`](days/day-01-baseline-and-the-log/LESSON.md).

Day 1 assumes nothing. It ends with a two-minute recording of you speaking badly, on purpose,
which is the most useful file in the whole project.

## The daily loop

```
1  Brief   — plan §8 tells you the day's title and concepts
2  Write   — /day-speak N
3  Speak   — the five blocks. Recorder on for the free rep.
4  Listen  — one playback. Anything you catch goes in docs/ERRORS.md.
5  Close   — tick the checklist, append the PROGRESS.md row
```

Closing is refused if the checklist is unticked or the recording does not exist. That refusal is
the only thing between you and a folder of days that look finished.

## Three commitments

**Record or it did not happen.** The gap between the sentence you meant and the sentence you
made is only audible on playback. That gap is the entire curriculum.

**Errors are the asset.** [`docs/ERRORS.md`](docs/ERRORS.md) is append-only and grows all
course. On Day 67 you read it and find four errors account for most of what goes wrong. Days 67
and 68 are written from your evidence, not from a syllabus.

**No streaks.** Miss a day and nothing happens. There is no counter anywhere. The next day is
still the next day.
