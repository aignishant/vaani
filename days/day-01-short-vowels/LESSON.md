## §1 Where we are

You meet a neighbour outside your home. They do not know your name yet. The speaking folder
helps you make a first message they can hear clearly. A greeting is like opening a door:
it gives the next exchange somewhere to begin.

You put your name inside a notebook. Someone finding it should be able to read who it belongs
to. The writing folder starts with the letters you need for that label.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** Play the recording. Can you hear your whole name and distinguish the intended
practice words? In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-change-one-sound.md), substitute a different
word on purpose. Mark that attempt RED for the intended word, then make and hear the repair.
If you cannot hear the difference, leave the check unfinished and try the pair again.

**Writing:** Compare your name with its usual spelling and case. In
[part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-repair-a-name.md), alter one starting letter on purpose.
Point to it, mark it RED, and restore it. If you accepted the changed form without noticing,
the check failed; run the check again.

Only record findings you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.
No listener check or learner result has been supplied with this lesson.
