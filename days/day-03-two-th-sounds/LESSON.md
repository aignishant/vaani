## §1 Where we are

You are at a shop counter. You say "thirty" and it comes out "tirty". The seller hears it. You
cannot yet make it. The speaking folder is about one small move: the tongue tip coming forward
to the teeth. Think of it as a door you open a crack to let the air through.

You leave a note on the kitchen table, and the person who reads it has to phone you to ask what
it means. The writing folder is about the two marks that tell a reader where a thought starts
and where it stops.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Making the th sound with the tongue inside, behind the teeth. If you cannot see it in the
  mirror, it is not there.
- Putting the tongue out for this but forgetting the buzz. Hand on throat.
- Letting the spelling decide. Both sounds are spelled t h. The word decides, not the letters.
- Full stops after every few words. A full stop ends a complete thing, not a breath.
- A small i for the word I. It is a capital every time.
