## §1 Where we are

folder is about two sounds that many languages keep as one: for one, the teeth touch the lip;
for the other, the lips push forward and the teeth stay away. Think of two doors on the same
wall: same room behind them, different handles.

You write a packing list for your brother and he packs one of everything. The writing folder
is about the end of a noun: the small -s that says "more than one", and the five words that
change their whole shape instead.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Making one sound for both v and w, because your first language has one. The mirror is the
  judge: teeth on the lip, or lips pushed out.
- Rounding the lips and also touching the teeth to the lip. Pick one.
- Trusting the number and forgetting the noun. "2 bag" says one bag to a reader.
- Adding -s to a plural that is already finished: childrens, peoples.
- Reading a list back by the numbers. Read it by the endings.
