## §1 Where we are

You stop a stranger on a new road to ask the way, and two of your words come out with a tongue
that taps. The speaking folder is about one part of the tongue in one part of the mouth: for
r it floats, for l it lands. Think of a foot on a stair: touching the step, or held just above
it.

You write a message to a new landlord, and every noun in it stands alone. The writing folder
is about the small words a, an and the, and the times when none of them belongs.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Tapping or rolling the r. Nothing touches. If you feel a tap, pull the tip back.
- Letting the l tip touch and leave at once. It lands and stays while the sound goes past.
- Choosing a or an by the first letter. Say the word; the first sound decides.
- Writing the before a general thing: "I like the tea." General means nothing in front.
- Reading your own message back in your own voice. Use the stranger's voice.
