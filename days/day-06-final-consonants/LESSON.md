## §1 Where we are

You are in a rickshaw on a noisy road, and the end of your word gets lost. The driver hears
half of it and has to guess. The speaking folder is about the last sound of a word: putting
the tongue where the ending is made, and keeping the buzz going when the ending needs it.
Think of a word as a step you have to put your whole foot on.

You write your first message to a group of new people, and every sentence is missing the
same small word. The writing folder is about am, is and are: the verb that joins you to what
you are, its short forms, and not.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Saying the ending loudly instead of putting the tongue in its place. The place is the
  rule; the loudness is not.
- Adding a small vowel after the ending: back-uh. That is an extra piece, not a finish.
- Cutting the buzz early on every word, so bad and bag become bat and back.
- Leaving be out because your first language can. On paper, it is never left out.
- Using are with one person: "my brother are". One takes is.
- Writing im, shes, dont. The apostrophe is small and it is looked for.
