# Day 8 · Word stress: the one syllable that carries the word

**Phase 1 — Unblocking** · Concepts: `PR-04` · Track: `PR`

## Why this day exists

Because a word with every sound correct and the stress on the wrong syllable is often not
understood at all, while a word with several sounds wrong and the stress right usually is. Of
everything in Phase 1, this is the highest-return single change.
