## §1 Where we are

It is your first week at a new job and the manager keeps saying "Go round." The speaking
folder is about the five sentences that introduce you, said in order, with a pause after each,
and with your eyes on the person instead of the page. Think of it as handing someone five
small stones, one at a time, so they can hold them all.

You have moved into a shared room and the things on the shelf need names on them. The
writing folder is about have and has: the verb for what you own, what you look like, who you
have and what is wrong with you today.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Saying the five in one breath because you are nervous. The listener catches none.
- Dropping I'm and I, so five sentences become five words.
- Learning the five so well that the pauses go. The pauses are part of what you learned.
- Writing has after I because it looks more careful. I always takes have.
- Writing am or is for an illness or a feature: "I am a cold." It is have.
- Losing the -s after a number: "two brother".
