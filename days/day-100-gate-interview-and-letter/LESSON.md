## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-answers-that-were-not-sentences.md), two strangers
hear the strength and *time when* answers, one in fragments on purpose and one from the gate take, and
say the claim, the evidence, and whether they'd meet you again. RED is when the gate take's stranger
can't name the evidence or scores 3 or below. GREEN is when they name both and score 4 or 5. To make it
go red on purpose, answer in fragments.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-letter-with-a-contraction.md), readers with the
gate wording mark every place a piece fails it. RED is when either fair copy gets any mark. GREEN is when
both fair copies get none and the broken copy gets at least four.

## §7 Traps

- A job that isn't real, so *why this job* fits any job.
- Answers as lists of fragments.
- A memorised answer read in a flat voice.
- A *time when* answer with no Result.
- Stopping and restarting the take.
- A transcript written from memory.
- *Dear Sir or Madam* with *Yours sincerely*.
- *I'm writing*, *I'd be grateful*: polite contractions.
- A possessive apostrophe changed by mistake.
- A greeting with no comma, or a first name only.
- All the checks in one read.
- Fair copies checked and never sent.
