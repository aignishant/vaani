## §1 Where we are

At a family dinner, your aunt starts a story with *The funny thing is, your uncle sat on the cake*, and
nobody laughs. Your cousin tells how she carried forty samosas across the city to a meeting that turned
out to be online, stops after the last four words, and the table laughs. The speaking folder is about
the anecdote: set up, build, land, and stop. You tell one true story three times, cutting each time,
then give the ending away and explain the joke on purpose, and let listeners show you where they smiled.

Your friend's essay ended with *In conclusion*, her thesis copied, her points listed, and a brand-new
idea about electric buses. The writing folder is about the conclusion: your answer again in new words,
your points pulled together, and a last line that says why it matters. You write the conclusion to your
essay, then write one with a new idea on purpose and let a reader find it.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- *The funny thing is, …* before the story starts.
- A set-up longer than the story.
- Details that don't make the ending funnier.
- Talking after the punchline.
- Explaining the joke.
- Laughing before your own punchline.
- The thesis copied word for word in the conclusion.
- The points listed again instead of pulled together.
- A new reason in the last paragraph.
- An apology in place of an answer.
- A so-what line too big to believe.
