## §1 Where we are

You have moved to a new part of the city and you need the chemist, the bus stop and the name
of the man who collects the rubbish. The speaking folder is about the three question words that
get you a name, a place or a person, and the falling voice that tells the listener you have
asked. Think of a question as a hand that goes up on the first word and comes down to point on
the last.

You are messaging a landlord about a room, and he keeps answering "Yes" to things you meant as
questions. The writing folder is about the word order that makes a question on paper: the verb
that jumps to the front, or the helper word that stands in for it.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- A what, where or who question with the voice going up. It sounds like surprise, not a
  question.
- One rising noun instead of a question. It gets "Yes".
- Who said with a w. It is "hoo".
- A statement with a question mark on paper: "The room is free?" The reader agrees with it.
- Do for one thing: "Do the room have". Does.
- The -s left on the main verb after does: "Does the room has".
