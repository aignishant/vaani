## §1 Where we are

Your neighbour's daughter had to redraw the weakest drawing in her art-college folder. She chose the one
she disliked most. Her teacher went down the college's list and found that a different drawing was the
one that missed a requirement. Her friend redrew a drawing better than before, but on a different task,
and it was sent back. The speaking folder is about the portfolio retake: checking every gate recording
against its own gate, retaking the weakest under its own rules, and letting a helper with only the gate's
wording check that the task didn't change.

Your uncle took every paper the bank asked for, all in one plastic bag, and was sent home to put them in
order with a list on top. The writing folder is about assembling the Writing Portfolio: every piece where
its row says, every row correct or corrected by a new row, and a reader's note on top. A stranger then
tries to find three pieces with a broken copy of the index and with the real one.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-retake-that-changed-the-task.md), a helper with only
the gate's wording checks take M and your kept retake in a hidden order. RED is when they find a
requirement missed in your kept retake, or nothing missed in take M. GREEN is when the kept retake misses
nothing and take M misses at least one.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-index-that-points-to-nothing.md), a stranger tries to
find three pieces with copy B and then with the real index. RED is when they miss even one piece with the
real index. GREEN is when they find all three with it, and got stuck at least once with copy B.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- Choosing the piece to retake by how you feel about it.
- Retaking a piece on an easier task, or with a requirement dropped.
- Counting turns or words during the take.
- Deleting or replacing an old gate recording.
- Giving the helper your requirement list instead of the gate's wording.
- Auditing the portfolio from memory.
- Editing an old row instead of adding a correction row.
- Making a missing piece today and filing it as old.
- A reader's note written to yourself, not to a stranger.
- Helping the stranger in the finding test.
- Pasting a deliberately broken index into the real ledger.
