## §7 Traps

- A yes/no question said flat. To the listener, nobody asked.
- A rise that comes back down on the last word. It sounds like surprise.
- "Yes." with no tail. It closes the talk.
- A tail that echoes the main verb: "Yes, I take." The tail echoes do, is or are.
- Not with no helper: "The cat not eat." Doesn't.
- The -s kept after doesn't: "doesn't likes."
- Two negatives on one line: "I don't have no bags." To a reader, you have bags.
