## §1 Where we are

You are cooking with your mother in a noisy kitchen, and some of what she calls across the
room arrives and some does not, and it is not chance. The speaking folder is about the beat
of an English sentence: the words that carry the meaning said loud and long, and the small
words between them said quick and quiet. Think of a sentence as a drum with a few hard hits.

Your friend is at the station gate trying to find your aunt from your message, and five words
with full stops were not enough. The writing folder is about the paragraph: several sentences
about one person, in the order a stranger builds a picture.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Every word the same weight. It feels careful. To a listener it is a robot.
- The small words stretched long: "thee", "mee". They should shrink.
- Tapping long words instead of meaning words. Length is not the rule.
- A list of words with full stops: "Short. Grey hair." No verbs, no picture.
- One sentence with nine ands. The reader loses the beginning.
- A sentence about the train inside a paragraph about the aunt.
- No step 4. Half the aunts in the world are short with grey hair.
