# Day 15 · Linking: where one word runs into the next

**Phase 1 — Unblocking** · Concepts: `PR-08` · Track: `PR`

## Why this day exists

Because a recording of separated words is the clearest possible signal that someone is speaking a
language they learned rather than one they use, and it is the cheapest of all the pronunciation
problems to fix. Every portfolio recording from here on is judged partly on whether the phrases
hold together.
