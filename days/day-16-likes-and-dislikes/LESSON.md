## §7 Traps

- Owning only two rungs, "I like it" and "I don't like it". The middle is where real answers
  live.
- "I can't stand it" about food someone cooked for you.
- An answer with no reason. The listener has nothing to hold.
- A reason that repeats the rung: "because I like it."
- A day on paper with no order words. The reader cannot tell your order from your list.
- An order word on every sentence. Signposts louder than the road.
- Finally in the middle. The reader thinks the day has ended.
- No feeling sentence. A timetable gets "ok" for an answer.
