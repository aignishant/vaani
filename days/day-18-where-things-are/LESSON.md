## §1 Where we are

A friend is in your kitchen feeding your cat and she cannot find the food. Everything you say
down the phone is true and none of it helps, because you never told her where to stand. The
speaking folder is about the anchor — one fixed spot — and the lap round the walls that a
listener can actually walk.

You are letting out a spare room and the card you put up at the shop starts with a blue lamp.
Somebody arrives and says "oh, it's smaller than I thought". The writing folder is about the
order a room has to come in on paper: the room, then the openings, then the furniture, then the
small things.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** Draw the room from the anchorless take in
[part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-tour-with-no-anchor.md), putting a **?** wherever you cannot
decide. Fewer than three **?** marks and the check is RED — either the broken take still had an
anchor hidden in it, or you drew from memory. Record it worse, or hand it to somebody else.

**Writing:** Answer the stranger's three questions from the shuffled, size-free version in
[part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-room-nobody-can-draw.md), naming the sentence each answer came
from. If all three are still answerable, the check is RED. Find out what is carrying them instead
of the order and write it down.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- Starting with *it's on the shelf* when the listener has never heard of the shelf.
- *There is* with two things, *there are* with one.
- Giving *left* and *right* from where you are standing, not from where they are.
- Jumping across the room mid-lap, so the listener turns their head for every object.
- *There's a lot of stuff there* — a sentence that places nothing.
- A written room that opens on a lamp, so the reader builds their own room round it.
- A description with no door and no window: nothing faces anywhere.
- No size word anywhere, so the reader uses the size of their own room.
- All good news and no honest negative line, which reads as an advertisement.
- Answering the read-back questions from memory of the room instead of from the page.
