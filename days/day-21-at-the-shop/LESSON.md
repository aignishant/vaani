## §7 Traps

- Starting the ask before the other person is listening: no *excuse me*, no half-beat of waiting.
- A flat request. *Can I have a bag please* with no rise is an instruction with a *please* on it.
- No close. The assistant is left not knowing whether you are finished.
- Politeness by length — three clauses of apology and the request lost at the end.
- Going silent when their answer is a question back, and then restarting the whole request.
- Taking *no* as the end of the exchange instead of asking what they do have.
- The apology chain for asking somebody to repeat. One *sorry* is plenty.
- Saying yes to something you did not understand, to end the difficulty.
- A sentence hidden in a list. A list is scanned, so the sentence is not read.
- A list with no quantities: the right things in the wrong sizes.
- Shorthand only you can read — *the usual*, *that one*, *Ma's*.
- A note with no name at the top. In a flat of three, *somebody* is nobody.
