## §7 Traps

- The bare item: *a coffee*, and four questions back.
- No quantity, so the server cannot tell one from a tray for four.
- Answering a one-word question with a whole rehearsed speech.
- Saying yes to the extra because *no, thanks* feels rude. It is not.
- An allergy mentioned after the food arrives instead of during the order.
- Ordering for somebody else who has not said what they want.
- Not asking what is in something, because asking feels like trouble.
- *Three milks*, *two rices*, *informations* — a plural on a noun that cannot take one.
- *An advice*, *a bread* — *a* or *an* in front of an uncountable noun.
- *How many bread* — the wrong question word.
- *There are some rice* — a plural verb with an uncountable noun.
- Writing the kitchen message from memory instead of from the shelf.
