**Phase 2 — The Toolkit** · Concepts: `PR-10` · Track: `PR`

## Why this day exists

Half of what you miss in real conversation is not vocabulary you lack — it is words you know,
wearing their spoken shape. Today you learn to hear that shape and to produce a handful of it, so
that the exchanges you record from here on stop having holes in the middle.
