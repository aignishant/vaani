## §1 Where we are

You are lost, you stop somebody, and two minutes later you are standing in the same spot with a
head full of information and no idea which way to start. The speaking folder is about the narrow
question that gets a short answer, and about the repeat-back — the one moment when being wrong is
still free, because the person who knows is still standing in front of you.

Somebody is coming to your place and you send them five instructions in one sentence. The writing
folder is about numbered steps with one instruction each, landmarks a stranger can actually see,
and the two lines everybody leaves out: the wrong turning, and what to do if you have gone too
far.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

stops. If the line completes, the check is RED — the answer was not hard enough, or you drew from

**Writing:** Follow the broken route in
[part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-step-with-two-things-in-it.md) literally and mark the lost
point. If there is no lost point, the check is RED: you are reading it with local knowledge. Then
restore the three things one at a time and record where the lost point moves.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- *Where is the hospital?* — an open question gets a description of the city.
- Holding up a phone instead of asking, so there is nothing to check and no second question.
- Asking somebody who does not know the area, who will often try anyway.
- Taking six steps when you can only hold two.
- Saying thank you instead of saying it back.
- A flat repeat-back, which is not a question, so you lose the correction.
- Nodding through the part you missed, which removes all the evidence including your own.
- Written directions as one sentence with five instructions in it.
- A step with two instructions: the reader does the first and forgets the second.
- A shop as a landmark. Shops close, move and take their signs down.
- No arrival line, so the reader stands in the right lane in front of four gates.
- Testing your own route by reading it forwards. You know the way; you will find nothing.
