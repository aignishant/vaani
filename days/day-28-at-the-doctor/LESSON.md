## §7 Traps

- *I have this since three days* — *since* takes a point in time, *for* takes a length.
- *I am not well* with no body part, no day and nothing you cannot do.
- A whole story before the first fact, so the listener holds it without knowing what it is about.
- The account with no ask, which ends with you in the corridor without the thing you came for.
- Saying *yes, OK* to instructions you did not follow.
- *Two blacks bags* — the plural *-s* on the adjective instead of the noun.
- *A bag black* — the adjective behind the noun.
- *A cloth black big bag* — material first, so the reader rebuilds the phrase.
- *Nice*, *good*, *very important* — opinion words that take nothing off the shelf.
- Six adjectives where two facts and one mark would have found the object.
- *The bag black* — the missing *is*.
- Commas sprayed between adjectives from different families.
