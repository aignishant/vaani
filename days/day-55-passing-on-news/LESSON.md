**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-news-buried-at-the-end.md), pass on one real piece of

from the first sentence.

## §7 Traps

- All the background before the headline.
- News with no source, which gets passed on as fact.
- Not stopping, so the listener never gets to react.
- Bad news said as brightly as good news.
- Guessing when you do not know: *he's fine, don't worry*.
- Repeating news the listener already has.
- A private matter passed on as news.
- The report's first sentence that sets the scene: *it was a normal Tuesday*.
- Opinion words in a report: *luckily*, *terrible*, *very*.
- No *when* or *where* in the first sentence.
- A report in time order, with the news in the last line.
