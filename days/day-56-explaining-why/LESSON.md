## §7 Traps

- *So* in front of the reason: *I was late, so the bus broke down*.
- *Because* and *so* in one sentence for one job.
- *That's why* with no reason said before it.
- A reason with no detail, which gets another *why?*
- Too many reasons, so none sounds like the real one.
- A swap that is believable, so nobody corrects it.
- *Before* and *after* swapped.
- *While* with a moment instead of a stretch of time.
- No comma after a time clause that comes first.
- A time clause left alone as a sentence.
- *Then* between every event, so no overlap shows.
