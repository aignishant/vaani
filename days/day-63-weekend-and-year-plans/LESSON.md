## §7 Traps

- Every plan in one form, so nothing sounds fixed.
- A fixed plan with no time or person.
- A hope said with *I'm* + *-ing* and a date.
- *I will* for every plan.
- *Hope* and *maybe* on everything, even the doctor's appointment.
- *Have went*, *have saw*: the second form after *have*.
- *She have*: *have* with he, she or it.
- *Haven't never*: the double no.
- *Have work*: the *-ed* missing after *have*.
- *No, I don't* as the answer to *Have you ever …?*
- A date in a present-perfect detail.
