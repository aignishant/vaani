## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-might-nobody-heard.md), play six real sentences, each
with the ladder word swallowed and pushed, to a listener who writes down how sure you sounded. RED is when
any pushed line is heard on the wrong step. GREEN is when every pushed line is heard right and at least
half the swallowed ones are heard as more certain.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-plan-with-no-who-and-no-when.md), give a reader your
real plan and another reader a copy with no names or dates, and ask them to place every step on a day
with a name. RED is when any step of your real plan can't be placed. GREEN is when all can, and the empty
copy's reader can place almost none.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- *Maybe* hiding a *probably* or a *probably not*.
- A swallowed *might*, heard as *will*.
- A swallowed *won't*, heard as *will*.
- *Probably* in the wrong place: *I will not probably*.
- No reason for anything below *definitely*.
- A plan with no names.
- A plan with no dates.
- *We* as the owner of a step.
- A wish step: *make sure it goes well*.
- A plan written as paragraphs, so nobody can find their line.
