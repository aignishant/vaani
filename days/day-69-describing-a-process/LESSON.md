## §7 Traps

- A process told as a stream of *and then*.
- No count, so the listener doesn't know how long to concentrate.
- A count that doesn't match the stages.
- No result, so the stages have no point.
- One stage that turns into a whole story.
- Counting the stages while you speak.
- The missing *be*: *The temple built in 1820.*
- The base form after *be*: *is make*.
- The wrong tense of *be* with a past date.
- *By* + doer on every sentence.
- A dropped *be* that turns a passive into a different active fact.
