## §1 Where we are

At a residents' meeting, a neighbour talks about parking while the water tank leaks, and your *No,
wait —* gets drowned out. The speaking folder is about getting in politely: a breath, a warm *sorry*,
a reason, a short point, and a hand-back. It's also about getting back on track with *anyway, as I
was saying*, and giving someone else their turn back with *you were saying?* It covers three real
interruptions in real conversations, and a deliberate barge-in tested on a willing partner.

At night you write a note to your daughter's teacher. In the morning, read aloud, it has a doubled
*a* and no time. The writing folder is about the second read: a gap, your voice, and three passes
from big to small. It covers a real letter edited into a clean copy, and a straight-after silent read
set against a stranger's marks.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- *No, wait —*: the barge-in, mid-word, with no *sorry*.
- A flat *sorry* that sounds like a formality.
- Getting in politely and then keeping the turn.
- Letting your own point go after someone interrupts you.
- Waiting for a gap so perfect it never comes.
- The second read done straight after writing.
- Reading silently, so missing and doubled words slip past.
- Fixing commas in a paragraph you're about to cut.
- Fixing in your head, not on the page.
- A new mistake made while copying out the clean copy.
