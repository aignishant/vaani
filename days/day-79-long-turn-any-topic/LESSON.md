## §1 Where we are

At a cousin's house, everyone draws a topic from a bowl. Your uncle starts on phones for children and
ends on hospital food. The speaking folder is about one frame you can pour any topic into: your point
in one sentence, two reasons with real examples, and back to the point. It covers three topics drawn
blind and said back by real listeners, and one turn that wanders on purpose until you find your
drift sentence.

The community newsletter asks residents what they think, and prints the letter that's a hundred and
fifty words. The writing folder is about four short paragraphs, *First of all*, *In addition* and *To
sum up*, with a word budget for each. It covers a real piece counted paragraph by paragraph and read
by someone who disagrees, and a padded copy that repeats itself up to the count.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- The wander: an example that turns into a story, then another.
- The point at the end, after the background.
- Reasons with no examples.
- Peeking at the slips and putting back the hard one.
- The same example for every topic.
- The view only at the end of the written piece.
- A hundred and fifty words in one block.
- A new reason in the closing paragraph.
- Cutting the examples to hit the count.
- Padding: *In my opinion, I personally think … very very …*
