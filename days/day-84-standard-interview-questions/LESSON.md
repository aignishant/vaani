## §1 Where we are

A bank help desk interview. *Why do you want to work here?* *Because it's a good bank.* *A strength?*
*I'm hardworking.* *A time you dealt with an unhappy customer?* *I always try to help.* Every answer is
polite and short, and the manager learns nothing. The speaking folder is about the shape each standard
question wants. You then run a full mock interview with a story bank, and finally dodge two questions
on purpose.

the writer actually did, and that's the one she's already called. The writing folder is about the
cover letter: four paragraphs, three jobs, inside the formal frame. You write one for a real advert,
then make a copy that could be sent anywhere.

## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-answer-to-a-different-question.md), two listeners
hear each question, then an answer, and say whether it answered. RED is when either says *no* or *half*
to one of your real answers. GREEN is when both say *yes* to both real answers, and at least one catches
a deliberate dodge.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-letter-that-could-go-anywhere.md), a reader decides
whether your letter could be sent unchanged for a different real job. RED is when they say *yes*. GREEN
is when they say *no* and name a word that stopped it, and the general copy's reader says *yes*.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- Right topic, wrong shape: a one-line answer with no example.
- *It's a good company*, with nothing after it.
- The fake weakness: *I'm a perfectionist*.
- A *time when* told as a habit: *I always …*.
- Stopping at the task and skipping what you actually did.
- One story used for every question.
- *Please find attached my resume*, as the whole letter.
- The letter with no job title and no place in it.
- Matches that are qualities with no proof.
- *I'd be glad*: a contraction slipping back in at the close.
