## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-too-soft-and-too-hard.md), two listeners score your
soft take, your hard take and your real complaint for *polite* and for *firm*. RED is when either scores
your real one at 3 or below on either. GREEN is when both score it 4 or 5 on both, and a deliberate take
scores low on one side.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-letter-written-in-anger.md), readers playing the
company fill four boxes and score how much they want to help. RED is when your real letter's reader
leaves a box empty or wrong, or scores help at 3 or below. GREEN is when every box is right and help is
4 or 5, and the angry copy's reader does worse.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- Three *sorry*s before the problem.
- *You sold me …*: blaming the person who can help.
- Describing the problem and never naming the request.
- Folding after the first *no*.
- Getting louder with each *no*.
- *Please do the needful*.
- A page of background and no order number.
- Two or three actions at once.
- Capitals, *!!!* and sarcasm.
- A threat in the first letter.
