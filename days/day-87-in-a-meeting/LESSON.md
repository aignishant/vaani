## §1 Where we are

A team meeting about the Diwali rota. You agree with half of it, don't understand one word, and have an
idea, and you say nothing because you can't find a way in. On Sunday, three people have three different
ideas of who's working. The speaking folder is about three short moves: agree and add, ask, and sum up
with names. You use them in a real meeting, then sum up vaguely on purpose and see what a stranger can
do with it.

At the cricket club, you take notes for the secretary, who's ill. You write down what people said, and
she phones to ask who's buying the balls. The writing folder is about notes for the person who wasn't
there: a header, numbered decisions, an actions table with owners and dates, and the next meeting. You
write them for a real meeting, then make a copy only you could read.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Silence all meeting, then a speech at the end.
- *I agree*, with nothing added.
- An *ask* that's really a challenge.
- A sum-up with *we* instead of names.
- A sum-up with no *Is that right?*
- Notes that record what was said instead of what was decided.
- An action with no owner, or with *soon* instead of a date.
- A header with no date and no apologies.
- *TBC* with no name and no date.
- Initials nobody outside the room can decode.
