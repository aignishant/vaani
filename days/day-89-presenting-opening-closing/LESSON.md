## §7 Traps

- Opening with an apology for your English.
- Starting on point one before the audience has arrived.
- A map with no number.
- A map whose names don't match the middle or the recap.
- *So … yeah … that's it*.
- A new point in the close.
- *Any-questions-no-OK*, with no pause.
- *…, however it …*: a comma where a full stop belongs.
- *Although …, but …*.
- *However* in every sentence.

One point is named rather than hidden. The comma-splice page says a comma between two sentences is
sometimes accepted when "the clauses are very short and alike in form" or the tone is "easy and
conversational". Formal writing is neither, so this course treats a comma before *however* or
*therefore* as an error in formal writing, which is what the dictionary entry for *however* also says.
