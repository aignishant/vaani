## §1 Where we are

At a family meeting about money, you read every bar on the electricity bill's graph, left to right. By
June nobody is listening, and your mother asks whether it's going up or down. The speaking folder is
about explaining a chart in four moves: what it shows, the big picture, one or two things to notice, and
what it means. You explain a real chart to someone who can't see it, then read every number on purpose
and see what's lost.

Your aunt's loan form asks for her sales in words. You write every monthly number, and the bank phones
to ask whether the business is growing. The writing folder is about describing numbers and trends: an
*Overall* sentence, direction and size words, and the small words *from*, *to*, *by* and *at*. You
describe real numbers so a stranger can draw them, then swap *by* and *to* on purpose.

Choose either folder first. Each stands on its own. Follow the parts in that folder's order.

## §7 Traps

- Reading every number, left to right.
- *As you can see …* before saying what the chart is.
- A big picture with no shape word.
- Numbers without units.
- No *so what*.
- Every number and no *Overall* sentence.
- *Rose by 420* when you mean *rose to 420*.
- *Rised*, *falled*.
- Mixed styles: *35%*, then *forty per cent*.
- A guessed reason written as a fact.
