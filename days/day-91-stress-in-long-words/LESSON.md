## §1 Where we are

At the college office, you ask about the *PHO-to-gra-phy* course, and the clerk sends you to the photo
studio next door. You said every sound right. The loud syllable was in the wrong place. The speaking
folder is about how the stress moves in a word family: PHOtograph, phoTOgraphy, photoGRAphic. The
endings tell you where it goes, and the vowels change with it. You check your own long words on
dictionary pages with audio, then leave the stress behind on purpose and see what a listener writes
down.

At the clinic, you write a waiting-room notice: *We tell all patients you must …*, and the doctor says
it sounds like shouting. The writing folder is about three formal jobs for the passive: rules, reports
and careful news. The form is *be* + the third form, and only *be* changes. You write a real notice,
then break it on purpose, with a missing *be* and a notice that hides everyone.

## §5 The check that must be able to fail

**Speaking:** In [part 1.3](parts/01-speaking/1.3-the-stress-left-behind.md), two listeners hear
sentences with long words, stressed wrongly on purpose or rightly, and write the word they heard. RED is
when either writes a wrong word, pauses or asks again for a rightly stressed sentence. GREEN is when every
right sentence is understood at once, and at least one deliberate one isn't.

**Writing:** In [part 2.3](parts/02-writing/2.3-the-notice-that-hid-everyone.md), two readers circle
errors in a notice and say what to do and who to ask. RED is when your real notice's reader circles a
real error, or can't answer. GREEN is when they circle nothing real and answer both, and the broken
copy's reader catches a form error or can't say who to ask.

Only record what you actually heard or saw. A deliberate exercise is labelled deliberate.

## §7 Traps

- *PHOtography*: the family's stress left behind.
- Every syllable equal, nothing weak.
- The right loud syllable, and the first vowel still full.
- Trusting the ending rule without checking: *Catholic*, *Arabic*.
- A tap on the right beat with a flat voice.
- *Payment must made*: the missing *be*.
- *The meter was check*: the wrong form after *be*.
- *It has been decided that …*: by whom?
- A notice with no contact line.
- All active, pointing at the reader; or all passive, with nobody in it.
