# Error ledger — Vaani

Append-only. **The most valuable file in this repository.**

Every day ends with one listen-back of the recording and one read-back of the page. Anything you
catch — a sound that came out wrong, a word said with the stress in the wrong place, a missing
-s, a sentence with no full stop — gets a row here. Not to punish; to see the pattern. By day 40
the same three mistakes will fill this table, and those three are your curriculum inside the
curriculum.

A row is never edited or deleted. A mistake you stopped making stays here, dated, as proof that
you stopped.
